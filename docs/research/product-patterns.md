# Product patterns, not efficacy rankings

Reviewed: 2026-09-29. These are documented affordances, not hands-on reacceptance of every current app/version. **HIGH** confidence applies only to what an accessible official document describes; transfer into our product is **LOW / HYPOTHESIS**. None of these pages establishes learning efficacy. No products are ranked.

## Evidence versus interpretation

Direct manipulation, Pinyin reveal/hide, teaching versus testing, honest raw ink, compact media controls and repeated multimodal practice are design options. Product prevalence does not establish a causal learning benefit. Their relevance must be assessed against [learning evidence](learning-science.md), [Learning Architecture](../LEARNING_ARCHITECTURE.md) and actual [observations](../PRODUCT_OBSERVATIONS.md).

Official blogs are used above only for product behavior/history. No retention percentage, efficacy claim, “AI accuracy” claim or optimal algorithm is accepted from marketing. For raw-ink/hint behavior, Skritter's detailed support pages provide stronger descriptive evidence than a homepage feature list.
